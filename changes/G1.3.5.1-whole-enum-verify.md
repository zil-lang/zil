# Whole body-local Enum replacement: independent Verify proposal

Design §8.1.5 requires mutable complete Sum replacement to prepare RHS before old cleanup, publish without recoverable failure, and preserve lexical shell age. The current Verify endpoint gate admits Managed<Copy Record> and the finite local Package profile, so a real whole Enum replacement is still closed. This change proposes only the G1.3.5.1 task's finite ordinary Enum endpoint; no source/runtime claim is made before actual producer handoff and tests.

Admission must derive a singleton body-local empty-path target from this verifier's actual address reconstruction, excluding parameters and nonlocal/projected targets. The paid structural pass accepts direct Managed<Copy Record> plus scalar/Unit fields, one Managed per owning arm and at least one owning arm overall. Nominal/type equality does not grant ownership or create permission.

At Commit, independently reconstructed complete RHS Sum content replaces old tag alternatives and leaf provenance exactly, rather than unioning old and new instances or simultaneously creating all variants. Preserve old complete shell debt and initialization age, consume the staged RHS once, and retire prior current-tag observations. Abort changes neither old responsibility nor tag epoch. Existing ordinary initialization/tag/loan/resource/cleanup checks continue to prove their own obligations; Analyze/Types proof functions are not called.

No public IR/Wire/schema/ABI extension, default-budget change, formal gate change or runtime allocation is proposed. New classification, Content copying and observation invalidation are prepaid; old paths retain existing fees. Actual Source interfaces, observed initial failures and exact final commands will be recorded compactly after execution; original logs remain local. Safety and cost comparisons with Rust, as well as product-level net breakthrough, remain unmeasured.
