# Preserve expression-site availability independently of declaration identity

An actual original free function can retain its declaration identity while expression #splice, provider expansion or statement emission replaces body nodes. The later closure-context producer must not treat nominal source identity as proof that a produced closure expression occurred in the original body. MetaIf/For statements also defer body selection/unrolling to Engine.

DeclarationOrigin gains ClosureSiteLineage::Original/Unavailable. Driver samples its own paid materialization-event revision at each actual original item visit, records changed original module/ordinal pairs, and marks the same retained row Unavailable when moving the row alongside that item. Nested body events mark their actual enclosing top-level row; existing generated/item-list loss of nominal lineage stays closed. Neither a diagnostic span nor source/name equality identifies the row. g10's producer must require Original before constructing a closure context.

The status changes availability only. It grants no source class, capture, target, loan, call or cleanup right. Nominal identity stays unchanged. New counters/changed-row storage and lookups are prepaid on actual tracked CT events; no-CT paths add no new logical work fee or changed-row allocation. Conservative creator-wide rejection is intentional until generated expression lineage has a complete producer.
