## Rust goals reflection

Evidence so far supports an explicit finite ownership protocol with exact nominal Store elements and real failure-preserving storage, including full unsigned values. This slice has not established Rust parity for the four project goals or a superior product outcome; closure and nested Product rows remain open until the independent kernels and native observations pass. The intended advance is ordinary data-model composition of these Stores without granting new StoreElement, array, callback-container, lending, or Share/Transfer qualifications.
