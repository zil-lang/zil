kernels. Existing limited-profile P0 acceptance is not invalidated by this new

The retained Source fixtures and `baseline.json` are real CLI observations, not
hand-built IR or inferred machine acceptance. Initial probe construction used a
wrong `record` keyword and omitted an expression-body terminator in three cases;
those parser failures were corrected before measuring the admitted/rejected
ownership boundary. They are not compiler defects or passing evidence.
