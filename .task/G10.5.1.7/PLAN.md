## Hypothesis
The destructor body of every owning environment is still expanded into its users even after repeated source exit tails have been shared. This exposes many correlated memory/branch states to LLVM and increases optimization/code emission work. Keep an internal owning-environment destructor boundary when a module contains at least 16 owning environment definitions. Empty/non-owning destructors and smaller modules remain unchanged. The static crossover avoids paying extra calls in small programs; it does not assert that cleanup is cold. This is a measured candidate, not permission to ship a slower runtime. Only the function noinline attribute changes, not its original instruction sequence, flags, capture order, or symbol identity.

A local LLVM17 screen suggested a useful trade-off but also found small cases exceeding 10% runtime cost. Those diagnostic measurements are NOT acceptance, do not count as LLVM23 results, and do not justify weakening the gate. Constant propagation of private live flags was separately screened and showed no clear improvement; it is not implemented.
