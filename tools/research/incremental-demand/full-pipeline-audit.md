The original `zil_db::ReactiveGraph` is still the BodyKey graph. The later stages now have their own actual function/dependency/result consumers and retained work products; this is **not** a claim that a single central reactive graph schedules every phase. `FunctionProduct` now has an actual canonical-publication consumer through its private current-result handle. The public digest and `AnalysisFunctionResult` inspection APIs still do not constitute a scheduler. Whole-project driver construction keys and conservative PrivateImplementation dependencies remain, as do some global traversals and supported-domain fallbacks. Consequently the implementation is not yet minimal and no blanket Rust parity claim follows.

Analyze still performs complete [raw admission](../../../crates/zil-analyze/src/budget.rs#L812), including recursive type/feature inventories, canonical IDs, aggregate quotas and instruction metadata. These checks also establish the current request's solver flags; they cannot simply be dropped on a local candidate hit. [CandidatePlan construction](../../../crates/zil-analyze/src/lib.rs#L100) still invokes the [canonical whole-module fingerprint](../../../crates/zil-ir/src/lib.rs#L2677); the 1 rebuilt function-query hash does not eliminate that pass. Global contracts and supported-domain fallbacks remain current. Existing counters do not divide the 26.87 ms between admission, identity hashing, those checks and materialization, so no individual time saving is inferred from this source inspection.

There are also definite copies after successful queries: [initialization reuse](../../../crates/zil-analyze/src/candidate_queries.rs#L292) clones [Initialization's nested block facts, predecessor vectors and reachability](../../../crates/zil-analyze/src/lib.rs#L374), and [Hold reuse](../../../crates/zil-analyze/src/candidate_queries.rs#L388) clones the complete `Vec<ValueHold>` before assembling the current candidate. Thus reuse counters do not mean shared candidate buffers or zero allocation. [Query finalization](../../../crates/zil-analyze/src/candidate_queries.rs#L411) rebuilds the current entry/result inventories, while [dependency checking](../../../crates/zil-analyze/src/candidate_queries.rs#L202) still constructs signature values for observed callees. These are concrete remaining costs for a later measured slice; sharing immutable result buffers with COW isolation would preserve their existing input/dependency and logical-budget proofs, whereas admission receipts would additionally have to replay current aggregate quotas, derived flags and original first-error fallbacks. Neither improvement is implemented or claimed by this audit paragraph.

The Rust comparison remains tied to the official commits and actual moved paths in [pipeline-audit.md](pipeline-audit.md#official-rustcargo-fixed-source-comparison): rustc `58cf8f9c35b355199727627d07ae34a4e2846d67`, Cargo `5f94df4789f005f9a352888e8355ffc645b7ed0e`.
